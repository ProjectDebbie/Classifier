A simple new visualization of exercise data discloses pathophysiology and severity of heart failure.
The complexity of cardiopulmonary exercise testing data and their displays tends to make assessment of patients, including those with heart failure, time consuming. We postulated that a new single display that uses concurrent values of oxygen uptake / ventilation versus carbon dioxide output / ventilation ratios ([Formula: see text]-versus-[Formula: see text]), plotted on equal X-Y axes, would better quantify normality and heart failure severity and would clarify pathophysiology.
