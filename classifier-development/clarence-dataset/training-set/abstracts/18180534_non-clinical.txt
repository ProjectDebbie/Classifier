Very late drug eluting stent thrombosis and cardiogenic shock: 1,249 days after
implantation.
P The marked reduction of in stent restenosis of drug eluting stents  DES  has
dramatically affected modern interventional therapy for coronary artery disease.
The promises of this potential panacea, however, have been recently attenuated by
the specter of late stent thrombosis. Stent thrombosis and in stent restenosis
reduction represent the double edged sword of neointimal inhibition.
