Corneal sensitivity changes in long term wearing of hard polymethylmethacrylate
contact lenses.
P The effect on corneal sensitivity of years of wearing hard contact lenses was
evaluated in 20 subjects who had worn polymethylmethacrylate  PMMA  lenses for up
to 20 years. An age  and sex matched group of 20 eyes was used as the control
group. It was found that corneal sensitivity diminished significantly in wearers
of PMMA contact lenses. This reduction of corneal sensitivity was found to be
most prominent centrally and was markedly related with the duration of PMMA
contact lens use. The loss of corneal sensitivity due to wearing contact lenses
increases the risk of a silent infection.
